01 / Selected Sessions

LOCAL MOMENTS.
GLOBAL AUDIENCES.

Intimate performances in unexpected places, from a tattoo studio to a taqueria, captured and distributed as a livestream content series together with media partner Beatport.

Places with a local identity

A taqueria, a tattoo studio, a skate venue. Each location gives the session a setting beyond the usual club environment.

Artwork people can take away

Selected Sessions artwork appears on posters and T-shirts, connecting the session with physical objects.

The product in the setting

El Tony Mate appears alongside the session artwork and merchandise, bringing the product into the same visual world.

Local talent / on stage and on screen

Local artists bring their city into the session.

Local artists played as support acts alongside the headline guests. Their contribution also continued beyond the sets: in short video series, they shared their favourite spots around their city.

These personal recommendations gave viewers a way into the local culture, adding something to discover beyond the music.

02 / DJ & Producer Academy

CREATE VALUE
FOR THE SCENE.

A talent development program that gives emerging artists education, mentorship and a real stage.

The reach built through Selected Sessions helped promote the Academies and their coaching opportunities for emerging talent.

Every edition draws more than 300 remix submissions. The Producer Academy in Ibiza brought selected participants together with coaches SKALA and Kid Simius for a week of studio work.

03 / Press

THE SCENE TALKS ABOUT IT.

Beyond the dancefloor, the formats earned 194 pieces of editorial and social coverage, from Complex and Notion to Glamcult, Numéro and DJ Mag.

As featured in

What made it work

Most brands show up when they want attention. El Tony Mate showed up with opportunities.

Instead of buying visibility, the brand invested in emerging talent and in experiences the scene actually wanted to be part of. Selected Sessions created the content and the reach. The Academy created lasting value for artists. Together, they made El Tony Mate a recognised part of the community.
